Questions & game rules

On one shared screen, the teacher records answers by clicking a choice. The first buzzer locks the question to that group. A wrong answer gives the other group a chance. If both miss, retry the same rock without moving. Only a correct answer earns a move. Each of the 25 rocks has its own fixed question. Questions flash automatically when a racer arrives.

Assign one group the horse and the other the car. Tap the buzzer buttons or use A / L. If the groups answer aloud, the teacher can click the first group’s buzzer on their behalf.
